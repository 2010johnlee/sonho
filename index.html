Who we are

Optical precision
since 2003

OptixCore is an independent manufacturer of precision optical components and thin-film coatings. We work with R&D labs, defense contractors, machine vision OEMs, and display manufacturers globally.

Our story

Founded in 2003 by a team of optical engineers from leading research institutions, OptixCore was born out of the need for an agile, high-precision manufacturer that could move at the speed of innovation.

Over two decades we have grown from a small polishing workshop into a full-service optical component supplier with advanced coating, inspection, and assembly capabilities under one roof.

We remain privately held, engineering-led, and obsessively focused on surface and wavefront quality.

Our values

  • Uncompromising quality — we inspect every surface, every time, with interferometry and profilometry before shipment.
  • Engineering partnership — we work alongside your design team, not just fulfill purchase orders.
  • Speed without shortcuts — rapid prototyping cycles that do not sacrifice specification compliance.
  • Sustainable manufacturing — energy-efficient coating processes and responsible material sourcing.

Quality & Metrology

Our capabilities

Where material science
meets precision optics

From raw glass blanks to fully tested assemblies, our in-house process chain gives us complete control over quality and lead time.

Glass Polishing & Shaping

CNC and traditional polishing to surface figure accuracies of λ/20 PV. We work with BK7, fused silica, N-SF11, CaF₂, and custom glass types.

λ/20 surface figure

Thin-Film Coatings

Ion-beam sputtering and e-beam evaporation chambers producing hard, durable multilayer stacks. AR coatings with R < 0.1% per surface across broadband.

IBS & e-beam PVD

Optical Films & Laminates

Proprietary multilayer polymer films for polarization control, privacy filtering, and broadband anti-reflection optimized for display and sensing applications.

Multilayer polymer

Precision Metrology

In-house Fizeau interferometry, profilometry, spectrophotometry, and laser damage threshold testing ensure every part ships in spec.

Fizeau / LIDT testing

Optical Bonding & Assembly

UV and optical cement bonding of prism assemblies and doublets. Clean-room assembly with wavefront error budgeting for the assembled system.

ISO 5 clean room

Custom Design Service

Optical design support using Zemax and Code V. We can co-develop from specification through prototype to production, including stray-light analysis.
